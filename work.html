Featured Project

Prima
Facie

Jodie Comer as Tessa Ensler

Jodie Comer starred as Tessa in Suzie Miller's one-woman play Prima Facie, directed by Justin Martin. The production originated in London's West End before transferring to Broadway.

The performance represents a major stage achievement in her career and is described by the official production as award-winning.

  • Tony Award — Best Actress in a Play
  • Laurence Olivier Award — Best Actress
  • Evening Standard recognition
  • WhatsOnStage recognition
