About

Jodie Comer

An English actress and producer whose work spans television, film and theatre.

Editorial portrait placeholder

Editorial Portrait

Jodie Comer began her professional acting career in television and gained recognition through roles including My Mad Fat Diary, Doctor Foster, Thirteen and The White Princess.

Her international breakthrough came with Killing Eve, in which she portrayed Villanelle / Oksana Astankova from 2018 to 2022. Her performance earned major recognition, including a Primetime Emmy Award and a BAFTA Television Award.

She subsequently expanded her film career with projects including Free Guy, The Last Duel, The Bikeriders, The End We Start From and 28 Years Later.

She also made a major transition to theatre with Prima Facie, playing Tessa Ensler. Her performance earned her major theatre recognition, including the Laurence Olivier Award and the Tony Award for Best Actress in a Play.
