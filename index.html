Introduction

An English actress from Liverpool, working across television, film and theatre.

Jodie Comer is an English actress from Liverpool known for her acclaimed work across television, film and theatre.

She rose to international prominence through her performance as Villanelle in the television series Killing Eve, earning a Primetime Emmy Award for Outstanding Lead Actress in a Drama Series.

Her career has expanded across major film productions and critically acclaimed theatre, including her award-winning performance in Suzie Miller's Prima Facie.

Prima
Facie

Jodie Comer as Tessa Ensler

Jodie Comer starred as Tessa in Suzie Miller's one-woman play Prima Facie, directed by Justin Martin. The production originated in London's West End before transferring to Broadway.
